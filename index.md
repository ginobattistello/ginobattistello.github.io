I study how **affective states** (mood and emotions) shape **creativity**. My work focuses on the value-based decision-making processes underlying **creative thinking**. I am particularly interested by how **subjective valuation** varies depending on context (task environment, internal states, etc.) and across pathological conditions such as **depression**. I have a trans-disciplinary approach, combining *experimental psychology*, *behavioral experiments*, *computational modelling* and *neuroimaging*.
