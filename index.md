I study how **affective states** (mood and emotions) shape **creative thinking**. My work focuses on
*subjective valuation*: how we judge the value of our own ideas, whether this process generalizes across domains,
and how it changes in conditions such as depression. I combine behavioural experiments, computational modelling
and neuroimaging.
